This website may use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage, and similar technologies.

This policy explains the types of technologies OLI may use and how visitors can control them.

## What Cookies and Similar Technologies May Be Used

OLI may use cookies or similar technologies for the following purposes.

### Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with the site, including page views, navigation patterns, referring pages, approximate location, device type, and general usage trends.

These tools help us improve the structure, content, and usability of the site.

### Functional Use

We may use basic functional cookies to support website behavior, remember preferences, maintain consistent page behavior, support embedded third-party services, or improve accessibility and usability.

### Donation and Form Services

Donation platforms, payment processors, form providers, email platforms, and related services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide their services, prevent fraud, maintain security, or remember information needed to complete a transaction.

### Outreach, Advertising, and Remarketing

OLI may use outreach, advertising, or remarketing tools to communicate with people who may be interested in OLI's mission.

These tools may use cookies, pixels, or similar technologies to measure the effectiveness of communications, understand referral sources, or display OLI-related messages on third-party platforms.

## Third-Party Services

Some website functions may rely on third-party services, such as analytics, embedded media, donation platforms, email tools, forms, social-media platforms, or advertising platforms. These services may use cookies under their own policies.

OLI does not control third-party cookie practices.

## Your Choices

Most web browsers allow you to control, block, or delete cookies through browser settings.

Some third-party platforms also provide account-level or device-level controls for advertising, personalization, and tracking.

If you disable cookies, some parts of the site may not function as intended.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the way cookies or similar technologies are used on the site changes.
